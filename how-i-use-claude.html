The Honest Part

I was skeptical for longer than I'd like to admit.

For months I watched colleagues talk about AI like it was changing everything, and I mostly nodded along while thinking: this probably isn't for me. I'm not a developer. I'm not a prompt engineer. I don't have time to learn a new tool when I'm already running at 100% on real deals.

What finally got me in wasn't a company mandate or a shiny demo — it was a specific deadline and a willingness to just try one thing. Not AI in the abstract. One conversation, for one account, with a clear goal.

What I found is that Claude works the same way a smart, fast colleague works. You give it context. You tell it what you're trying to accomplish. You push back when it's off. And the output is something you can actually use — not a draft you have to rewrite from scratch.

"I didn't need to become a different person to use this. I just needed to stop treating it like software and start treating it like a conversation."

Building things I couldn't build before

The Framework

Four things I do every single time.

This isn't theory. It's the actual pattern I follow — figured out through doing, not reading about AI.

Step 01
📂

Fill the Room

Give it everything. Discovery screenshots. The actual PDF. Brand assets. Call notes. Your org chart. Claude can handle more context than you think — and more context means better output.

Step 02
🗣️

Think Out Loud

Don't ask for deliverables yet. Explain the situation. The account. The stakeholders. What you're trying to accomplish and why. Treat it like a briefing, not a search query.

Step 03
🔄

Push Back, Redirect

The first output is a starting point, not a final answer. Tell it what's off. Ask it to go deeper on one section. Say "that's not quite right, here's why." It gets better fast.

Step 04
✅

Ship Real Work

The goal isn't a draft to rewrite — it's something that goes directly into the deal. A live demo. A polished deck. A playbook your whole team can use. Real output for real stakes.

Where to Start

You don't need to overhaul anything.

Start with one thing. The ramp is faster than you think — and the trepidation disappears after your first real output.

📂

Start with a document you already have

Take a recent RFP, a 10-K, or a lengthy briefing doc and paste it in. Ask Claude to summarize the top 3 business priorities and flag any risks you should know about. That's it. See what comes back.

🎯

Try the briefing, not the prompt

Instead of "write me a slide," try: "I have a meeting with [company] on [topic]. Here's what I know. Here's what I'm trying to accomplish. What should I say first?" The framing changes everything.

🔄

Push back once before you accept the output

Whatever Claude gives you first — tell it one specific thing that's off. "The tone is too formal," "this doesn't account for X," "give me a sharper angle on the intro." One iteration makes a big difference.
