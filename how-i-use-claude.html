CBRE / GWS

CBRE is the world's largest commercial real estate services firm. The Global Workplace Solutions team had a high-stakes demo coming up — and the standard demo environment didn't tell the story they needed to tell.

Live Interactive Demo BOE Deck
What I Fed Claude
📸Screenshots of their existing FM platform
📝Discovery call notes and stakeholder themes
🎯The story I needed to tell in the demo
🏢CBRE GWS portfolio context and scale
"I didn't give it a spec. I gave it screenshots and a narrative goal. That's when I realized you don't need to know how to build things — you need to know how to describe them."
The Journey
1
I started with screenshots, not a spec

Dropped in screenshots of their current platform and asked Claude to understand what it was and what the user was trying to accomplish in it.

2
Described the story, not the UI

Told Claude what the ideal day-in-the-life looked like for a GWS facility manager — the outcomes they wanted, not the screens they needed.

3
Iterated on UX flow and narrative

Pushed back on what to show first, how to make interactions feel modern, which data points would land in the room.

4
Tuned the data to match their portfolio

Made the demo feel like it was built for CBRE — their terminology, their scale, their operational reality.

Equinox

Equinox is one of the most recognized premium fitness and lifestyle brands in the world. The FY27 SIC — Strategic Investment Council — was approaching. This is the highest-stakes internal meeting of the year: you're pitching the investment case for your account to Salesforce leadership.

Live Demo SIC Deck Executive POV
What I Fed Claude
🏋️Equinox brand and business context
💼Current deal status and stakeholder map
📋SIC format and what leadership expects
🤖Agentforce capabilities relevant to fitness/luxury
"The SIC prep used to be a Sunday project. This became a Tuesday afternoon. And the story was better — because I asked Claude to challenge my assumptions first, before building the slide."
The Journey
1
Started with what Equinox actually cares about

Before building anything, I asked Claude to play back Equinox's business priorities. Not the CRM pitch — the real business priorities. That grounded the whole narrative.

2
Built the arc: why now, why Salesforce, why Agentforce

What's the transformation story? How does AI fit the Equinox brand and member experience? Claude helped make the case feel inevitable, not pitched.

3
Slide by slide — talking points, not just content

Built not just what to say, but how to say it for a Salesforce leadership audience. The inflection points. The proof points. The questions they'd ask.

4
Built the interactive demo for the meeting itself

Asked Claude to turn the key concepts into a live, clickable experience. Went from "can you make this interactive?" to a shareable demo in one session.

Service Logic

Service Logic is a Bain Capital portfolio company — 60 operating companies, $1B+ in HVAC and mechanical services revenue, and heading toward an IPO. They issued a formal FSM RFP. They had already rejected ServiceMax. The bar was high and the stakes were real.

Complex RFP SE Playbook Solution Architecture
What I Fed Claude
📄The full 100+ page RFP document
⚠️Why ServiceMax was previously rejected
🏗️Context on their 60-OpCo multi-entity structure
📊ASC 606 / IPO readiness as a known Must-have
"A 100-page RFP is just a conversation waiting to happen. Claude reads faster than I do and it doesn't miss things I'm tired of reading. The ASC 606 gap came up in the first five minutes."
The Journey
1
Full RFP analysis: strong, gap, or ISV needed

Asked Claude to map every requirement to one of three categories. It caught things I would have glossed over — and flagged them in terms of deal risk, not just feature coverage.

2
ASC 606 surfaced immediately as the critical gap

Claude flagged that this is exactly why ServiceMax lost, identified it as an IPO-driver Must-have, and suggested leading with RightRev as the named ISV — with CBRE as a reference.

3
Built the SE Playbook: what to say, what to avoid

Not just the solution — the positioning. How to frame our gaps confidently. How to differentiate from ServiceMax. The language that lands with a PE-backed leadership team.

4
Solution Architecture for a 60-OpCo structure

Mapped Salesforce Revenue Cloud + RightRev + FSC to their actual organizational model. Built the "This Week's Actions" doc so the team had clear next steps.

📋

GSA PBS

GSA Public Buildings Service is the federal government's real estate arm — managing billions in property across the country. Federal accounts have specific procurement rules, formal stakeholder hierarchies, and very little tolerance for a pitch that doesn't feel tailored to government reality.

Presentation Deck Full Talk Track
What I Fed Claude
🏛️GSA PBS account context and mission
👥Attendee list with roles and seniority
📐Meeting agenda structure and time constraints
💡Notes on what had resonated in prior meetings
"I've done hundreds of presentations. But having a talk track written for THIS specific room, with THESE specific stakeholders — including how to handle procurement questions — changed how I walked in."
The Journey
1
"What should actually happen in the first 20 minutes?"

That was my first question. Claude pushed back immediately: who are the decision-makers in the room? What does each of them need to walk out believing?

2
Built a government-specific narrative arc

Lead with mission impact, not product features. Federal is different — the language, the proof points, the ROI framing. Claude knew this without being told.

3
Slide-by-slide talk track with transitions and pauses

Not bullet points to read off — actual language. Specific transitions between slides. How to handle the inevitable procurement question. Where to pause for reaction.

4
Turned it into a printed guide for the table

The guide went with me into the room. Not to read verbatim — to walk in knowing exactly what I was going to say before I said it.

Related Rentals

Related Rentals is part of The Related Companies — one of the country's largest private real estate owners. They needed to see Agentforce working in a context that felt real for a property management business, not a generic product demo.

2 Live Interactive Demos Agentforce
What I Fed Claude
🏘️Related Rentals company profile and portfolio
😤Core pain points: tenant comms, vendor mgmt, leases
👤Day-in-the-life of a property manager persona
🤖Where Agentforce should visibly improve the workflow
"Two fully interactive, account-specific demos — no dev team request, no 2-week wait. Just a conversation that got specific enough. The second demo came from one sentence: 'now show what happens when Agentforce touches this.'"
The Journey
1
Anchored on one relatable workflow

"A property manager shouldn't need 4 systems to answer a tenant's maintenance question." That sentence became the demo's entire narrative hook.

2
Built the CRM Console demo first

Showed the data model and the service case flow — what the world looks like today, before Agentforce. A realistic baseline they'd recognize.

3
"Now show what happens when Agentforce touches this"

That was the entire prompt for the second demo. Claude built the Agentforce experience from one sentence — because the context was already established.

4
Team tested both before the meeting

Shared the live demos with the AE and internal team for review and feedback. Made tweaks in the same session — no handoffs, no waiting.

Hertz

Hertz is one of the world's most iconic rental car brands — 100+ years old, operating at massive scale, and in the middle of a significant transformation. They needed to see Agentforce not as a new AI tool sitting next to their existing systems, but as something living inside the screens their service agents already use every day.

Live Interactive Demo Agentforce
What I Fed Claude
🟡Hertz brand colors (iconic yellow #FFD100, black)
🎫Service agent workflow context and case types
🚗Real use case: damage dispute, rental return scenario
🎯The story — AI in the flow of work, not a separate tool
👤Specific stakeholder context (Lauryl)
"The story wasn't 'here's what AI can do.' It was 'here's what Agentforce looks like in the exact screens you already use.' Showing it inside their world — not ours — completely changes the conversation."
The Journey
1
The concept: show the before, not just the after

Instead of jumping straight to the AI-powered state, I asked Claude to help design a three-act reveal. Start where Hertz already is. Earn the transformation.

2
Built the three-stage narrative structure

"Governed Foundation → AI in the Flow → One Platform." Each stage a click. Each click a visible shift — from greyscale and locked data to glowing Agentforce yellow.

3
Made it real with a specific service scenario

A Tesla damage dispute at LAX return. Real case type, real priority queue, real data fields unlocking as Agentforce comes online. Not abstract — recognizable.

4
"Revise based on the story — and get the Hertz branding right"

That was my note heading into the final iteration. Claude dialed the Hertz yellow into every detail — the nav stripe, the glow, the case accent colors — until it felt unmistakably Hertz.
