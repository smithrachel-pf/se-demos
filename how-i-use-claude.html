the thinking sessions.

techniques
⏱time-boxing
artificially constraining scope to force the AI to prioritize ruthlessly
"let's say i only have 10 minutes — what are the most compelling stories?"
removes the AI's default instinct to be comprehensive. forces a judgment call, not a list.
✓promise-check
auditing the AI's output against actual customer commitments before delivery
"review these dry run notes against what we promised the customer — what are we missing?"
catches the gap between what was sold and what's being built — before the customer does.
📐prior work as template
feeding your own best previous outputs as the creative and structural standard
"for inspiration, review my CBRE Savannah canvases"
teaches style, not just content. the AI learns your standard — not a generic one.
⚠contamination flagging
explicitly naming what context should NOT bleed into the output
"the visuals I included are from ANOTHER client — ensure none of that intel carries over"
AI context windows blend sources by default. naming the boundary prevents silent hallucination contamination.
↺iterative refinement
treating first output as a draft, not a deliverable — always planning a correction pass
"review this canvas with an editorial eye" / "make suggestions for where i can strengthen this"
the first answer sets the floor. the second prompt raises the ceiling. first draft is never final.
⇌cross-source synthesis
pointing the AI at every relevant source simultaneously — channels, DMs, Drive, PDFs, call notes — in one prompt
"review the channel, DMs with Jackie and Aida, the Google folder, and this RFP"
the AI's output quality is directly proportional to input surface area. narrow inputs produce narrow outputs.
