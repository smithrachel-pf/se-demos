The broker's real workflow

Five modes, constantly.

Every leasing broker operates across five modes. The problem: most live in Mode 4 (executing active deals) and starve Modes 1–3. The result is a feast-or-famine pipeline that compounds over 18+ months.

1

Business Development

Finding new tenants, outbound outreach, referral cultivation, first meetings

Starved when deals are active
2

Pipeline Nurturing

Lease expiration tracking, drip content, re-engaging dormant contacts, timing triggers

Starved when deals are active
3

Market Intelligence

CoStar / CompStak research, vacancy and comp tracking, sector news, competitive positioning

Starved when deals are active
4

Active Deal Execution

Space criteria, market surveys, RFP/LOI, financial analysis, lease closing

Where brokers live today
5

Client Stewardship

Post-deal check-ins, renewal reminders at 18-24 months, referral asks, portfolio advisory

The feast-or-famine trap: When you're deep in Mode 4 deals, Modes 1–3 go dark. Eighteen months later, the pipeline is empty. Top tenant reps aim for a 100-prospect active pipeline at all times, with 3–5 active prospects needed to close 1 deal per quarter. The AI teammate's job is to keep Modes 1–3 running even when the broker is buried in active deals.
